# Product: JW Group

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Prospective customers choosing a business within JW Group and contacting that business.
- Job seekers reviewing opportunities and submitting applications.
- Website administrators maintaining business content and organizational information.

## Product Purpose

Present JW Group's businesses, leadership, work, and opportunities so visitors can find the right business and contact it or apply for a job. The confirmed success measure is monthly submitted contact forms and job applications.

## Positioning

JW Group brings together five business areas: real estate, hospitality, veterinary services, health products, and construction. The website provides a single entry point to these businesses, not a separate selectable organizational entity named JW Group.

## Operating Context

Visitors explore the business profiles, company background, leadership, organization charts, news, vacancies, and contact information on desktop and mobile browsers. Administrators maintain published information through the existing protected management area.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve all five businesses: JW Real Estate, 12 The Residence Hotel, 3DPet Hospital, JW Herbal, and Thanabul Property.
- Organization charts represent individual businesses and provide hierarchy exploration, branch expansion, person details, and relationship paths; the detailed real-estate chart includes zoom and export controls.
- Business content and organization records are centrally maintained. Presentation changes must not invent employees, reporting relationships, awards, testimonials, or business claims.
- JW Real Estate's website is https://www.jwrealestate.co.th.
- Thai is the primary language; the established site also supports English, Chinese, Korean, Japanese, and Russian.
- Years follow the site's Buddhist Era convention.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the JW Group name, official logo assets, and established corporate real-estate identity. The current structure-page brief explicitly requests navy/orange. Future work must distinguish a route-specific request from a change to the whole brand.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing business profiles and images, executive records, organizational nodes and departments, company timeline, awards, news, careers, and contact information. The running site and existing content are implementation evidence, not permission to invent additional facts. Founding dates and legacy quantitative claims should be verified against approved records before changing them.

## Product Principles

- Help visitors reach the correct business rather than treating every business as interchangeable.
- Make corporate relationships legible without changing the underlying reporting structure.
- Keep contact and recruitment paths accessible on desktop and mobile.
- Use maintained records and official assets as the source of factual content.
